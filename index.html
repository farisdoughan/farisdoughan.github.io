Working on understanding diffusion models. Advised by Dr. John Vastola.
I am studying the theory of diffusion models, a class of generative models that produce new samples by learning to reverse a gradual process that adds noise to data. I am interested in the foundations of this process, using tools in probability, stochastic differential equations, and score-based modeling help explain why these models learn meaningful data distributions and when they generalize.
