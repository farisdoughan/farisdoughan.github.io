A minimalist student site for mathematics, probability, and related areas.

I’m an undergraduate at Harvard studying Mathematics and Statistics, with a secondary in Computer Science. I also hope to pursue the concurrent master’s in Applied Mathematics.

Broadly, I’m interested in the mathematics behind probability and statistics, stochastic processes, and applications to nearby fields such as theoretical computer science, finance, and games.

I’m also interested in the mathematical and combinatorial properties of random processes, including questions involving graphs, matrices, and related structures.

Outside of class, I enjoy playing poker and shooting trap and skeet.
