I am a second-year undergraduate at Harvard College, concentrating in Mathematics and Statistics with a secondary in Computer Science. I also hope to pursue the concurrent Master’s in Applied Mathematics.

As an undergraduate, I am still exploring different areas. Some things I’m currently interested in:

  • Mathematics. Probability theory and stochastic processes; real analysis and PDE theory; random structures (matrices, random walks, graphs, etc.) and their combinatorial properties.
  • Statistics. High-dimensional statistics; machine learning theory; mathematics of data science.
  • Computer Science. Theory of computation; computational methods used across these fields.

This summer, I will be researching diffusion models, advised by Dr. John Vastola in the Computational Neuroscience Lab.

Feel free to watch some of my talks (some of which were recorded), as well as my teaching experiences. You can also find a detailed breakdown of my technical coursework.
