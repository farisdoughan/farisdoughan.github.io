I'm an undergraduate at Harvard studying Mathematics and Statistics, with a secondary in Computer Science. I also hope to pursue the concurrent master's program in Applied Mathematics.

Broadly, I am interested in the mathematics behind probability and statistics; stochastic processes and their applications to areas such as theoretical computer science, finance, and games; and the mathematical and combinatorial properties of random processes involving objects such as graphs, matrices, and related structures.

Outside of class, I enjoy playing poker and shooting trap and skeet.
